Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

How do I install my eSIM?
After purchasing an eSIM package:
  1. Go to "My eSIMs" tab
  2. Tap on your purchased eSIM
  3. Tap the "Install eSIM" button
  4. Your eSIM will be installed instantly - no QR code needed!
When does my eSIM activate?
Your eSIM data plan begins when you first connect to a mobile network in your destination country. The validity period starts from that moment, not from the time of purchase.
Can I get a refund?
Yes! If you haven't used any data from your eSIM package, you can request an instant refund through the app. Go to "My eSIMs" and select the unused package to request a refund.
How do I check my data usage?
Open the app and go to "My eSIMs". Select your active eSIM to see real-time data usage, remaining data, and expiration date. We make tracking your usage simple and transparent.
Is my phone compatible with eSIM?
eSIM is supported on:
  • iPhone XS, XR, and newer models
  • iPad Pro (2018 and later), iPad Air (2019 and later), iPad mini (2019 and later)
  • Most modern Android devices (check your device settings)
To verify: Go to Settings → Cellular/Mobile Data → look for "Add eSIM" or "Add Cellular Plan" option.
Can I use my regular SIM and eSIM at the same time?
Yes! Your phone supports dual SIM functionality. You can keep your home number active for calls and texts while using the eSIM for data. This way, you stay reachable without expensive roaming charges.
What if I run out of data?
You can purchase a top-up package or a new eSIM plan directly in the app. Installation is instant, so you'll be back online in seconds!
Which countries do you support?
SimSignal offers eSIM coverage in 168+ destinations worldwide. Browse available countries in the "Store" tab of the app to find the perfect plan for your destination.
My eSIM isn't connecting. What should I do?
Try these troubleshooting steps:
  1. Turn on "Data Roaming" for your eSIM in Settings
  2. Turn Airplane Mode on, wait 10 seconds, then turn it off
  3. Make sure your eSIM line is selected for Cellular Data
  4. Restart your device
If issues persist, contact us at support@simsignal.com with your order details.
